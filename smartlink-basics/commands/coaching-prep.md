# /smartlink:coaching-prep

Prepare coaching talking points for a 1:1 meeting with a sales rep.

## Steps

1. Ask for the rep's name or ID
2. Pull `get_deals` filtered to this rep
3. Pull `get_activity_rate` for their activity levels
4. Pull `get_deals_at_risk` for any of their deals at risk
5. Calculate rep-level metrics vs team averages

## Output Format

**Coaching Prep — [Rep Name] | 1:1 on [Date]**

**Quick Snapshot:**
- Pipeline: $X across Y deals
- Activity: X activities this week (vs Y average)
- Win Rate: X% (trailing 90 days)

**What's Working:**
- [Genuine positive]

**The ONE Thing to Coach On:**
[Single most impactful improvement area]

**Why This Matters:**
[Connect the metric to business impact]

**Conversation Starters:**
- "[Open-ended question about the focus area]"
- "[Follow-up question that goes deeper]"

**Deals to Discuss:**
1. [Deal name] — [Why it needs discussion]
2. [Deal name] — [Why] (max 3 deals)

## Coaching Approach

- Start positive. Acknowledge what's working first.
- One thing at a time. Don't overwhelm.
- Ask, don't tell. Help the rep self-discover.
- Make it specific. Numbers and examples, not generalities.
- Agree on one action. End with a specific commitment.
